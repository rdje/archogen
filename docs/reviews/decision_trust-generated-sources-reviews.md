## The fact / decision

`M3.6.6.1`'s acceptance is a round, by a context that did not write the record, finding no defect
(`decision_executable-design-reviews.md`'s closure rule). Each round is a new context, read-only, given the record;
`decision_trust-inventory.md`; `ROADMAP.md` §4.4 and §14.4; the instrument it extends — `xtask/src/trust.rs`,
`xtask/src/trust_gate.rs`, `trust/roots.eadl`; and the review method. It is asked for inputs with no outcome or two,
ways a generated source's provenance escapes the gate, contradictions with the parent record, hand-offs that are not
precise or not sufficient, false claims of fact, and ambiguities two implementers would resolve differently.

## Rounds

**Round 1**, `2026-10-06`: the reviewer re-measured the census — 57 files, 56 the roots', the harness's own one
beside them — and applied the marker rule word for word to them at `HEAD`, finding none marked and three marker phrases
past line 20 inside string literals. There were 25 findings, 19 of them defects. The largest: keying the shared item
on a generator *and* an input missed §14.4's literal case, a shared input through two generators, and §4.4's, a shared
generator over two inputs (R1-4); the rules said "root" where the comparison harness and handed run-time data count
too (R1-2, R1-3); a chain of generators lost its first link (R1-6); a checker compiling what the generator's own code
wrote made no item (R1-7); and the record changed the parent's closed lists and case 5 without amending them (R1-15,
R1-16). Every finding is answered in the record; R1-21 is declined there, with its reason.
