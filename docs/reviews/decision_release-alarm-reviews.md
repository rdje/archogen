# Review history — `decision_release-alarm.md`

The independent review of [`docs/decisions/decision_release-alarm.md`](../decisions/decision_release-alarm.md) (leaf `M4.1.6`), a design with no code yet, before `M4.1.7` and `M2.21.2` build on it. Each round is a reader who had not written it, asked whether a timer route as a declared source is sound against the variant and the composition as they stand, whether the release timer as a requester holds against the joint rules, and whether any question its leaf asks is left unanswered.

**Round 1** — `2026-11-06`, leaf `M4.1.6.1`: not acceptable, 13 findings, 9 defects, all this design's but for one older row, as a first round of a design is. The decision that stood: a timer route as a declared source, the source term sound given a true separation, measured at a bound of 111 against 104 by a timeline; the rejection of an amended condition 6; the order, every key a time and none reading `J`; a node's call as a primitive's span; the system's own level refused. What fell was how much the first draft said needed no work: "nothing changes" where a rate stood for a separation, a route for an alarm, and a role the composition did not have.
