  ### Why two sources and not one

  §13.4 asks for the fixture "through **both** an independently specified event timeline **and**
  the engine's accounting path". Here the first is the roadmap's own table, parsed, and the second

  simulator was written from the operational rules above it — so their agreement is evidence
  rather than a tautology. That they agree interval-for-interval on the first run is the strongest
  single result in the analysis story so far.
