- ID: `M1.22`
  Status: `done`
  Goal: settle by measurement whether LinkedSpec's `SExprDocumentV1.spec` can serve as a **third
  independent recognizer** of archogen's normative surface syntax (`docs/semantics/grammar.md`) — the
  question `M1.11` raised and `M1.14` closed, on the single ground that no complete-input mode
  existed. `M1.20.5` removed that ground: the document route returns every top-level form of
  archogen's real four-form description and rejects leading, intervening and trailing input with a
  typed error and no partial value, on archogen's own frozen probes.
  Reproduce / issue: `M1.14`'s verdict was "the shipped grammar cannot be archogen's reader nor an
  independent cross-check … the route stays open behind an upstream complete-input mode." That mode
  now exists and is `verified` at the adopted pin. Leaving the closure standing would mean the
  normative grammar keeps exactly two independent opinions — archogen's reader and archogen's
  conformance suite, which are the same codebase's two views — when a genuinely third one is
  available for the cost of a harness.
  Acceptance: a differential harness runs archogen's reader and the vendored recognizer over a named
  corpus (the S0 fixtures, the semantic corpus, the grammar conformance cases) and reports agreement
  or disagreement per document, as a development-time tool with **no dependency added to the engine
  workspace** — the recognizer is invoked as an external process, per the zero-dependency decision
  and §4.4; a ratchet-style test fails if a previously agreeing document starts to disagree, and
  never passes by deletion; the comparison is scoped to what archogen's grammar actually defines
  (token boundaries and document completeness), **not** to eADL semantics, and the leaf says so
  explicitly, because the recognizer classifies `-1` as a number while eADL's exactness rules are
  archogen's own; every divergence found is enumerated and classified as archogen stricter, the
  recognizer stricter, or a genuine mismatch needing a decision; a written verdict lands in the leaf
  and in the book chapter that carries the verification story, since the book is the director's only
  view of this.
  Priority: **medium** — it is the third opinion `M1.11` asked for, now unblocked. It does not gate
  `M1.12` or `M1.13`, and it must not be started before `M1.20` closes, because its corpus and its
  build depend on the state that tree leaves behind.
  **Verdict, `2026-09-30`: the third opinion is real, and it earned its place on its first run.** Over every tracked
  description, 119 of 120 agree with archogen's reader on their forms. The one that does not is `LS-007`'s evidence,
  where the recognizer refuses and archogen accepts. The grammar-derived recognizer sides with LinkedSpec: archogen's
  reader is more permissive than `grammar.md`, which neither of archogen's two opinions had seen. Filed as `M1.37`.
  Verification: see the checklist — `scripts/third_opinion.sh` with nine arms; the first record written and checked;
  the ratchet shown firing on the real corpus.
