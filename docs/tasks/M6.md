## Goal

Demonstrate that the program's central hypothesis holds in practice: three materially
different systems from one catalog release, genuine composability of imported sub-HW and
sub-OS descriptions, two distinct extension experiments, and a measured reuse baseline
against the frozen unseen evaluation cases.

## Non-Goals

- No blanket claim that any future feature requires zero engine work (§12 M6 exit).
- No economic extrapolation from three cases (§16).

## Acceptance Criteria (roadmap exit gate, §12 M6)

- Composability and extension have executable demonstrations.
- Previously qualified descriptions retain their meaning under locked versions.
- Reuse measurements have a baseline.
- Successful rejection of unsupported requests is reported, without counting rejection alone
  as successful generation.
